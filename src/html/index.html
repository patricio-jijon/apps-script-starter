Washington Heights STEM Center.

Located inside the New York Public Library Washington Heights branch, the NYC FIRST STEM Center supports school communities and FIRST teams with hands-on learning in robotics, coding, engineering, electronics, and digital fabrication.

Field trips, community workshops, open hours, credit-bearing classes, FIRST LEGO League, and FIRST Tech Challenge are part of the Center's programming. Valid NYC FIRST Member Cards are required for open hours and community workshops.

Built to connect with the school year.

The catalog can be mapped to scope & sequence, learning objectives, grade bands, and standards instead of being a collection of disconnected one-off activities.

🤖 Robotics & FLL

Mechanisms → sensors → block coding → autonomous challenges → engineering documentation.

🧊 Design & Fabrication

Sketching → CAD → 3D printing → vector design → laser cutting → prototype evaluation.

🌎 Environmental Engineering

Energy systems → solar testing → environmental sensing → data → community infrastructure proposals.

💻 Coding

Algorithms → events → loops → variables → debugging → robotics and physical computing.

🔌 Physical Computing

Circuits → sensors → actuators → Arduino-style logic → responsive prototypes.

🧪 Chemistry Engineering

Matter → reactions → variables → measurement → process improvement and safe testing.

🎛 Sound Engineering

Waves → frequency → electronics → speakers/microphones → sound effects and digital audio.

📐 Standards Mapping

Activities can be aligned with relevant New York State science, computer science, math, engineering, and arts learning standards.
